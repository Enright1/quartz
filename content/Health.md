🖱️ [*Toxinless.com* by Dan](https://www.toxinless.com/)

A "directory of foods and supplements without questionable additives". Contains many good ideas on how to source high quality health related stuff. Includes many specific product recommendations, but is very US-focused. Worth checking the comment sections too.

🖱️ [*haidut.me* by Georgi Dinkov](https://haidut.me/)

I recommend putting your health related search terms in here and reading whatever you find. Good analysis of various medical research. Very heterodox views. Ideas you will be hard-pressed to find elsewhere. A lot of research around cancer. Also has a product page.
