Here’s a potential **cross-country RV itinerary from Austin to San Francisco**, optimized for scenic drives, iconic attractions, and manageable daily driving times. I’ve adjusted stops for logical flow, added key detours, and flagged optional/skippable spots:

---

### **Route Overview**

**Austin, TX → Dallas/Fort Worth → Amarillo, TX → Trinidad, CO → Denver/Boulder → (Aspen) → Rapid City, SD → Yellowstone → Grand Teton → Salt Lake City → Moab, UT → Monument Valley → Grand Canyon → Sedona → Phoenix → San Diego → Los Angeles → Las Vegas → Death Valley → Yosemite → Lake Tahoe → Reno → San Francisco**

_(Total driving time: ~80–90 hours | Ideal duration: 4–6 weeks)_

---

### **Stop 1: Austin, TX → Dallas/Fort Worth, TX**

**Drive:** 200 miles (~3.5 hours)  
**Highlights:**

- **Austin**: Breakfast tacos, Barton Springs Pool, South Congress Avenue murals.
    
- **Waco** (optional stop): Magnolia Market (for _Fixer Upper_ fans) or the Texas Ranger Hall of Fame.
    
- **Dallas/Fort Worth**: JFK Memorial, Fort Worth Stockyards.
    

---

### **Stop 2: Dallas/Fort Worth → Amarillo, TX**

**Drive:** 365 miles (~5.5 hours)  
**Highlights:**

- **Cadillac Ranch** (Amarillo): Spray-paint the iconic buried cars.
    
- **Palo Duro Canyon** (30 min detour): Hike or bike the "Grand Canyon of Texas."
    

---

### **Stop 3: Amarillo, TX → Trinidad, CO**

**Drive:** 260 miles (~4.5 hours)  
**Highlights:**

- **Capulin Volcano National Monument** (NM): Hike the rim of an extinct volcano.
    
- **Trinidad, CO**: Explore the quirky **A.R. Mitchell Museum** or hike at **Trinidad Lake State Park**.
    

---

### **Stop 4: Trinidad, CO → Denver/Boulder, CO**

**Drive:** 200 miles (~3.5 hours)  
**Highlights:**

- **Garden of the Gods** (Colorado Springs, optional): Red-rock formations.
    
- **Denver**: Red Rocks Amphitheatre, Union Station, craft breweries.
    
- **Boulder**: Flatirons hike, Pearl Street Mall.  
    
---

### **Skiers’ Detour**
From Denver, drive to **Aspen** (4 hours) for slopes, then rejoin the group in Salt Lake City.

---

### **Stop 5: Denver → Rapid City, SD (Mt Rushmore)**

**Drive:** 400 miles (~7 hours)  
**Highlights:**

- **Carhenge** (Alliance, NE): Quirky Stonehenge replica made of cars.
    
- **Badlands National Park** (SD detour): Martian-like landscapes.  
    **Stay Focused:**
    
- **Mt Rushmore**: Evening lighting ceremony.
    
- **Crazy Horse Memorial**: Ongoing mountain carving.
    
- **Custer State Park**: Wildlife loop for bison herds.
    

---

### **Stop 6: Rapid City → Yellowstone NP**

**Drive:** 500 miles (~8 hours)  
**Break It Up:**

- **Devils Tower** (WY): Sacred monolith (1.5-hour detour).
    
- **Bighorn Mountains** (WY): Scenic drive on US-14.  
    **Yellowstone Highlights:** Old Faithful, Grand Prismatic Spring, Lamar Valley for wolves.
    

---

### **Stop 7: Yellowstone → Grand Teton NP → Salt Lake City, UT**

**Drive:** 320 miles (~6 hours total)  
**Must-Stop:**

- **Grand Teton NP**: Jenny Lake hike, Mormon Row barns.
    
- **Salt Lake City**: Temple Square, Antelope Island State Park (bison).
    

---

### **Stop 8: Salt Lake City → Moab, UT**

**Drive:** 230 miles (~4 hours)  
**Highlights:**

- **Arches NP**: Delicate Arch, Devil’s Garden.
    
- **Canyonlands NP**: Mesa Arch at sunrise.
    

---

### **Stop 9: Moab → Monument Valley → Grand Canyon**

**Drive:** 400 miles (~7 hours total)  
**Break It Up:**

- **Monument Valley** (AZ/UT): Guided Jeep tour through iconic buttes.
    
- **Horseshoe Bend** (Page, AZ): Short hike for Colorado River views.  
    **Grand Canyon South Rim**: Mather Point, Bright Angel Trail.
    

---

### **Stop 10: Grand Canyon → Sedona, AZ**

**Drive:** 110 miles (~2 hours)  
**Highlights:**

- **Oak Creek Canyon**: Scenic drive on AZ-89A.
    
- **Sedona**: Cathedral Rock hike, vortex sites, art galleries.
    

---

### **Stop 11: Sedona → Phoenix, AZ**

**Drive:** 120 miles (~2 hours)  
**Optional:** Skip Phoenix unless you need supplies. Instead, detour to **Saguaro NP** (Tucson) for giant cacti.

---

### **Stop 12: Phoenix → San Diego, CA**

**Drive:** 360 miles (~6 hours)  
**Highlights:**

- **Joshua Tree NP** (detour): Hidden Valley Trail, Cholla Cactus Garden.
    
- **San Diego**: Balboa Park, Coronado Beach, tacos in Old Town.
    

---

### **Stop 13: San Diego → Los Angeles, CA**

**Drive:** 120 miles (~2.5 hours)  
**Highlights:**

- **Laguna Beach** (detour): Tide pools and art festivals.
    
- **LA**: Griffith Observatory, Venice Beach, Getty Center.
    

---

### **Stop 14: LA → Las Vegas, NV**

**Drive:** 270 miles (~4.5 hours)  
**Highlights:**

- **Seven Magic Mountains** (NV): Colorful desert art installation.
    
- **Vegas**: Strip lights, Bellagio fountains, Fremont Street.
    

---

### **Stop 15: Vegas → Death Valley → Yosemite NP**

**Drive:** 450 miles (~8 hours total)  
**Break It Up:**

- **Death Valley NP**: Badwater Basin, Zabriskie Point.
    
- **Mammoth Lakes** (CA): Hot springs or June Lake detour.  
    **Yosemite Highlights**: Tunnel View, Half Dome, Mariposa Grove.
    

---

### **Stop 16: Yosemite → Lake Tahoe, NV/CA**

**Drive:** 180 miles (~4 hours)  
**Highlights:**

- **Tioga Pass** (seasonal): High-altitude scenery.
    
- **Lake Tahoe**: Emerald Bay, hiking or kayaking.
    

---

### **Stop 17: Tahoe → Reno → Sacramento → SF**

**Drive:** 220 miles (~4 hours total)  
**Skip Unless:**

- **Reno**: “The Biggest Little City” (casinos, Riverwalk).
    
- **Sacramento**: Old Town or farm-to-table dining.  
    **Final Stretch**:
    
- **Silicon Valley** (optional): Apple Park, Computer History Museum.
    
- **San Francisco**: Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz, Muir Woods.
    

---

### **Key Adjustments**

1. **Skip**: Wichita Falls (little to see), Phoenix (unless resupplying).
    
2. **Add**: Grand Teton, Moab, Monument Valley, Joshua Tree, Death Valley.
    
3. **Seasonal Note**: Tioga Pass (Yosemite) and mountain roads may close Nov–May.
    

---

### **Scenic Drive Highlights**

- **US-550** (CO): “Million Dollar Highway” between Ouray and Silverton.
    
- **Pacific Coast Highway** (CA): Swap LA→SF for coastal views if time allows.
    
- **Beartooth Highway** (MT/WY): Near Yellowstone for epic mountain vistas.
    

This route balances natural wonders, quirky stops, and iconic cities while keeping drives under ~8 hours (most 4–6).
